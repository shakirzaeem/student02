Technology in Action

AI is not only for big tech companies. Here are four places I have seen it myself or read about.

Education

Learning apps adjust the difficulty based on how you answer. AI tutors can explain a topic in different ways until it clicks. The catch is that students still need to do their own thinking, or the learning does not stick.

Business

Shops use AI to guess how much stock they will need, and banks use it to catch fraud. Many small businesses use chatbots to answer customers after office hours.

Healthcare

Doctors can use AI to help read scans such as X-rays, which can speed up the first check. The doctor still makes the final decision, because patient safety comes first.

Daily life

  • Maps apps predict traffic and suggest faster routes.
  • Streaming apps recommend shows.
  • Phone keyboards predict the next word.
  • Voice assistants set alarms and answer questions.
