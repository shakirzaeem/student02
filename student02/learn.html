Learn: The Basics of AI

What is AI?

AI is software that can learn from data and then make decisions or create things without being told every single step. When you get a movie suggestion or your phone unlocks with your face, that is AI at work.

Main features

  • It learns from examples instead of fixed rules.
  • It can handle huge amounts of data quickly.
  • It improves when it gets better data.
  • It can work with text, images, sound and numbers.

Advantages

  1. Saves time on repeated tasks.
  2. Works all day without getting tired.
  3. Can find patterns that people miss.
  4. Gives quick first drafts and ideas.

Limitations and challenges

  • Mistakes: AI can sound confident and still be wrong, so I always double-check.
  • Bias: if the training data is unfair, the results can be unfair too.
  • Privacy: some tools store what you type, so never paste secrets.
  • Jobs: some tasks will change, so people need to keep learning.
